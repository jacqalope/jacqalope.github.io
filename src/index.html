About

What kind of writing and why?

Technical writing stumbled onto me by serendipitous chance. I grew up multilingual, and was contacted one day for a project that included Taiwanese and Japanese content.

I always loved studying different languages and cultures. This is partly where I get my passion for interrogating the best ways to communicate. Did you know that the word "No" also means nothing and zero (0) in Vietnamese? There are so many ways to communicate that we've never dreamed of.

So perhaps it's no surprise that I found it deeply satisfying to come up with elegant solutions for communicating complex subjects. Technical writing quickly led to UX writing and documentation of APIs as well, and UX writing has led to content design. Why? Because Writing is Designing.

A combination of good design and writing means better customer experiences.
